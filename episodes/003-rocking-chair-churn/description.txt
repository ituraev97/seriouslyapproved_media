In 1913 the U.S. Patent Office approved a rocking chair that churns butter: you rock, a ratchet turns, and a barrel churn spins beside you. Then meet its relatives — five patented ways to make a rocking chair power a fan, from a "noiseless" corkscrew rod to a porch fan that keeps the flies away. Real drawings, quotes word for word.
