This invention relates to fan attachments
for rocking-chairs; and the object thereof is
to provide an. improved device of this class
which is simple in construction and operation
and which is adapted to be connected with an
ordinary rocking-chair; and with these and
other objects in view the invention consists in
the device hereinafter described and claimed.

the lower end of said rod should possess con-
siderable weight in order that the device may
properly operate, the downward movement
of the rod being accomplished by gravity only.
