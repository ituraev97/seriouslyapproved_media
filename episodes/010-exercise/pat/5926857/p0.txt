[57] ABSTRACT

An armor with rollers is provided that enables a user to move
in all positions by rolling on a hard and smooth surface while
constantly varying his bearing points on the ground. The
armor includes a pair of gauntlets extending from beyond
the user’s hand to the user’s elbow and having rollers at both
ends thereof. The armor also includes a pair of rigid leg pads
having rollers near the user’s knees.
