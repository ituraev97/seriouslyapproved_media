EDWARD TERRY, OF BRIXTON, ENGLAND.

1,252,628.

To all whom it may concern:

Be it known that I, Epwarp Terry, a
subject of the King of Great Britain, resid-
ing at Milton Hall, 117 Loughborough
Park, Brixton, England, have invented a
new and useful Improvement in Coat-Rugs,
of which the following is a specification.

My invention has reference to equipments
and accoutrements for personal wear of the
kind which may be worn as a coat cloak or
wrap or utilized as a tent or shelter.

Among the objects I have in view is to
provide an improved device of this charac-
ter which while being extremely simple in
construction and cheap in manufacture may
be easily converted into a variety of articles
of personal wear or equipment with a mini-
mum. of trouble and without the employ-
ment of extraneous aids except such as are
likely to be readily at hand. Moreover my
invention contemplates a device of the afore-
said kind which when worn as a coat or
cloak shall be neat in appearance and serv-
iceable to the ends in view.

These and such other objects as may here-
inafter appear or are incidental thereto are
attained in the manner which I will now
proceed to describe in detail for the ready
understanding of which reference should be
