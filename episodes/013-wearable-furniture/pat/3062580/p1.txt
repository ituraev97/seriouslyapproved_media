An existing problem to many persons engaged in sport-

when it is necessary to sit for long periods of time on a
‘ough or uneven surface which may be damp or cold.
(n view of this problem, it is the primary object of the

ising it leaves the seat on which he was sitting.

Another object of the present invention is to provide a
seat pad having a clip connected thereto which may be
quickly attached to the belt, pants, skirt, or any similar
article of apparel making it easy to be worn by men,
women, or children as desired.

A further important feature of the present invention is
10 provide a seat pad of the character described in the

wet seats or ground dampness, with the interior of the
device being constructed of a foam rubber or the like,

Another feature of the invention stemming from its
