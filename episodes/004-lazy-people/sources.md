## Quotes and where they are
### US 4,091,805 — Vibrating back scratcher (scan p2 = text page, col. 1–2)
- "An artificial hand" … "has a plurality of fingers bent in scratching position and a wrist" — description, col. 1
- "of any suitable material such as, for example, plastic" — description, col. 1
- "which is inexpensive in manufacture" / "used with facility, convenience and safety" / "functions efficiently, effectively and reliably to scratch the back of a user" — background (objects), col. 1
- "for vibrating the rod in axial directions" — description, col. 1
- "so that it is readily extended to an electric wall outlet for the recharging power" — description, col. 2
- "I do not wish to be limited thereto, for obvious modifications will occur to those skilled in the art" — col. 2
- Facts: rod with the hand on one end, hollow handle on the other; electric vibrator (preferably an electromagnet) and rechargeable battery in the handle; ON-OFF switch; FIG. 1 shows it in use; 2 claims, 4 figures.

- "approximately twenty centimeters long" / "Of course, these dimensions are not critical" — description, col. 2

### US 2,941,346 — Remote control of a lawn mower (scan p4–p7, text)
- "Remote control, requiring positive and certain action, in general involves complex instrumentation." — col. 1
- "positive control combined with simplicity in instrumentation is a goal that has not heretofore been readily achieved" — col. 1
- "does not require complex instrumentation, separate control circuits, or the use of radio" — col. 1 (continues "…or super power frequencies")
- "preventing entanglement of the mower in the cable strands" — col. 4 (cable 75 is reeled in as the mower approaches the control station)
- "may be confusing" — col. 4 (left/right control)
- "Mere rotation of control knob 13c will cause corresponding "rotation" of the mower." — col. 7 (quotation marks around "rotation" in the scan)
- "automatic grass hunting system" — col. 7
- "The mower will thus guide itself around a given plot" — col. 7
- "When the entire area has been cut the mower will merely propel itself in a tight circle" — col. 7
- Facts: electric mower fed by a cable from control station 13 (box 13a, power switch 13b, knob 13c); steering by releasing drive to the left or right wheel via solenoids; signal sent by slightly modifying the power voltage; feeler arms 145/146 sense uncut grass. Filed 1953, issued 1960. 19 claims, 14 figures.

### US 5,540,186 — Mechanically assisted animal self grooming device (scan p4–p5)
- "all of these devices require human assistance to be useful" — background, col. 1
- "Most animals will remain appreciative for extended periods of time, when brushes are moved against their bodies by the human hand." — summary, col. 1
- "to simulate the brushing movements of the human hand" — summary, col. 1 (col. 2 has "the brushing movement of a human hand")
- "Another object is to encourage the animal to spend more productive time with the moving brushing or rubbing medium of the device" — summary, col. 1
- "protecting the living environment from unnecessary animal hair" — summary, col. 1
- "Yet another object is that the present invention produces a synergistic effect." — summary, col. 1
- "harmless to an animal upon chewing into the line cord" — description, col. 2
- "at a movement of 10 to 15 revolutions per minute" — description, col. 2
- "a lure 32 is mounted atop a straight, flexible spring 30, that is constantly moving in circles" — description, col. 2
- "sounds of a mouse, or a human talking to an animal" — description, col. 3 ("prerecorded, intermittant sounds of…")
- "as an incentive for an animal to frequent the device more often" — description, col. 3
- Facts: motor turns an upright bristle brush (rubbing medium 26); base with slotted openings for loose hair, flea eggs and dander, carpeted floor, debris retention rail; optional sensor (motion, heat or optical) starts it; FIG. 1a lure drawn as a mouse.
