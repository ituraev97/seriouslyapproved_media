[57] ABSTRACT

A chair with facilities providing refreshment and relax-
ation and more specifically includes a chair having an
armrest with a beverage can or bottle cooler incorpo-
rated therein operatively associated with a refrigeration
system and a small refrigerated compartment is pro-
vided in the vertical side portion of the chair below the
armrest for refrigerated storage of refreshments or edi-
ble products in position for easy access to the occupant
of the chair.
