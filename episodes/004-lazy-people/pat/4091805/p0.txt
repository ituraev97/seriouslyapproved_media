[57] ABSTRACT

An artificial hand having a plurality of fingers bent in
scratching position, is affixed at the wrist to a first end
of an elongated rod. An electric vibrator is coupled to
the other end of the rod in a hollow handle accommo-
dating such end of the rod. The vibrator is energized by
a battery to vibrate the rod and the hand in axial direc-
tions.
