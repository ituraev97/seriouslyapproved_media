When my openers are to be used as fixtures
on benches or counters, a wide flat handle is

A screw-hole with a transverse web a’ and

The centering gage, with its gaging contacts
b%, 68, and fulerum contacts 64, 64, are as be-
fore described, and the same is true of the

it is only the central portion of either of the
lips shown, that need actually engage witha
sealing cap, and-hence it will be seen that the

It will be obvious that while it is highly con-
ducive to efficiency to have two gaging con-
tacts, one at each side of the cap engaging lip,

if. the other contact and one of the fulerum
contacts be removed, as indicated by the dot-
ted line in Fig. 1.
