[57] ABSTRACT

A domestic appliance for drying vegetables comprises
a basket rotatable in an open-topped container which
can be closed by a cover from the underside of which
there is suspended a rotatable element capable of ro-
tation by a crank and a set of gears carried by the
cover. A peripheral rim of the rotatable element and
the upper edge of the basket form complementary
coupling means whereby the basket is rotatable in the
container on turning of the crank.
