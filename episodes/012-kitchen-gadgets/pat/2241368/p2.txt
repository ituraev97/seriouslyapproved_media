This invention relates to a filtering device

and more particularly to a coffee maker and tea.

maker. ‘Though the filtering device to be de-
scribed may be used with facility in the making
of coffee and tea, it is not.limited to such use
and may be generally used in the laboratory to
effect filtering processes.

The filtering device is a combination of a

filter’ funnel adapted to hold a paper filter and
a bottle-like flask: adapted to receive filtrate.

‘These two elements are formed integral with

each other or are joined to form a single ele-
ment.

“In the development of the device it was found:

that the mere combination of a funnel with a
pot or. flask for the filtrate did not effect the
results desired for such a combination was tnop-
erative unless means were provided to permit the
escape of air from the pot or flask as the air
is displaced by the filtrate. This finding was
unexpected for the necessity for the air pas-
sage was created ‘by the simple joining of ‘the
filter funnel to the filtrate flask. In prior prac-
tice, when an ordinary filter funnel is associated
with a filter flask, even in those cases in which

‘the filtrate flask has a widened.neck to support

the separate filter funnel, as a practical mat-
ter there was sufficient leakage between the fil-
ter funnel and the neck of the filtrate flask. to
allow the. displaced air to escape.
