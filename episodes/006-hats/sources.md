### US 4,080,665 — Combination tennis racket cover and cap or hat (scan p3–p4, text)
- "Various types of caps and hats are, of course, also well known." — prior art, col. 1
- "is considered to be entirely unique" — prior art, col. 1 ("The combination of the herein invention, that is, a combined tennis racket cover and cap or hat is considered to be entirely unique.")
- "a completely new and unique article" — summary, col. 1
- "the user need only detach a part of the tennis racket cover from it to be provided with a hat or cap suitable for play" — summary (objects), col. 1
- "It has a crushable crown part" — description, col. 2
- "to provide an adjustment for head size" — description, col. 2 (snap fasteners 44/46 and 48/50)
- "from coming in direct contact with the springs of the racket" / "when the cap may be moist with sweat or the like" — description of FIG. 6, col. 3 (flap 60)
- "the purpose of the draw string being to be able to collapse the crown to a flat condition" — description of FIGS. 7–9, col. 3
- "The combination constitutes a single article but yet has all of the utility and conveniences of both" — col. 4
- Facts: cover in two parts; second part is a cap (bill + crown) attached by a zipper around the bill; hat version (brim + crown) attached by a zipper around the brim; FIG. 3 shows the cap worn; 11 claims, 9 figures.

### US 3,908,199 — Fan hat apparatus (scan p3–p5, text)
- "Folding hand fans are well known." — background, col. 1
- "useful for holding in a hand and moving back and forth to propel air currents toward the face" — background, col. 1
- "Usually the folding fans are highly decorative." — background, col. 1
- "Use of the fans has been limited to propelling air and to decorations." — background, col. 1
- "Wide use of the highly collapsible nature of the fans has not been made." — background, col. 1
- "The present invention creates from a hand fan a unique readily collapsible hat." — summary, col. 1
- "Snap-type fasteners are used in a preferred embodiment to give positive locking of the brim edges." — summary, col. 2
- "fan hat 1 when partly spread operates as a fan" — description of FIG. 2, col. 3

- "Crown 72 of the hat is formed of adjacent flat ribs and is well vented." — description of FIG. 7, col. 4

- "even an unexperienced helmsman" — background, col. 1
- "whether the helmsman of the boat has the hat properly positioned on his head" — objects, col. 2
- "be of simple construction and substantially automatic in operation" — objects, col. 2
- "LEAVING HARBOR" / "RED RIGHT RETURNING" — tags 28 and 30, description, col. 3
- "through the upper lens portions of a pair of glasses worn by the wearer of the hat" — col. 3
