[57] ABSTRACT —

A tennis racket cover constructed of a fabric material or
the like and having at least a partial peripheral zipper.
One side of the cover is formed at least partially as a cap
or hat. The cap or hat has a peripheral zipper for secur-
ing it to the other part of the cover. The one part of the
cover may be formed as a cap with a crown and a bill or
it may be formed as a hat with a crown and a brim.
When formed either way, the cap or hat is provided
with an adjustment as to size.
