-An object of the invention is that of pro-

ter a period of time determined by one cycle
of operation of an apparatus in the circuit.

A further object is that of providing a

alarm at a set hour, close an electrical cix-
cuit to a lamp and to an article heater, and
open said circuit when an article has been
heated and again give a second alarm after
the article has been heated.
