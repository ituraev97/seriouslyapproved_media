Be it known that I, ERNst WILHELM ORTH,
cutler, a citizen and a resident of Hamburg,

tain new and useful Improvement in Egg-

My invention relates to improvements in
egg-cups, by which the removal of the egg-
shell is facilitated, and by which the cup is
made adaptable to the size of the egg.

The egg-cups at present in use, and made of
porcelain or metal, do not adapt themselyes
to the shape of the egg, and the removal of
the egg-shell is often somewhat troublesome.

The accompanying drawing represents an
egg-cup, the upper part or vase of which is
made of flexibiestripsa. ‘These strips can be
drawn together by pushing upward a ring B,
and thus be made to hold the egy ¢ firmly.

The egg-shell is afterward ejected by the
upward pressure of a plate d which slides ver-
tically with its rod e in the stem f of the egg-
cup under the influenceof aspringh. <A but-

ton gis provided to release the plate and rod
and allow of the spring to force it upward.

Having now described my invention, what
I claim as new, and desire to secure by Letters
Patent, is—

An egg-cup, comprising a vase formed of
separate, flexible strips, a ring surrounding 30
said vase and adjustable thereon for drawing
the strips inwardly, a supporting standard
having a hollow stem, a rod extending through
said hollow stem and provided with an ejector
plate at its upper end and a button at its
lower end, and a spring located between the
standard and the button on the ejector rod,
substantially as shown and described.
