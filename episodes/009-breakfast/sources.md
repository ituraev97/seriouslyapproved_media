## Quotes and facts per patent

### US 2,513,839 — Bacon cooking aid (drawing sheet, Fig. 1)
- "curling and crimping of bacon strips during the cooking operation" — description (objects)
- "under the action of the weight, so that substantial pressure is supplied to the bacon" — description
- "permits cooking from both sides at the same time and with better contact" — description
- Facts: a flat metal platen on a crooked (bent) handle lies on the bacon in a frying pan; the bent neck rests on the pan's rim; a weight near the platen presses the strips flat. Filed Nov. 30, 1945; issued July 4, 1950 (Fourth of July).

### US 510,085 — Egg-Cup (drawing sheet, single figure)
- "My invention relates to improvements in egg-cups" — description, opening
- "the removal of the egg-shell is often somewhat troublesome" — description
- "The egg-shell is afterward ejected by the upward pressure of a plate" — description
- Facts: upper part = vase of flexible strips; a ring slid up the strips squeezes them around the egg; a button at the bottom of the hollow stem pushes an ejector rod/plate (with a spring between standard and button) up through the cup to eject the shell.

### US 886,266 — Egg-opener (Sheet 1 Fig. 1; Sheet 2 Fig. 3)
- "the purpose of the invention is to provide a very simple and economic device for opening eggs" — description, opening
- "adapted to pierce the shell of the egg laid on the cradle" — description
- "and in so doing will throw off the empty shell." — description, operation
- Facts: two hemispherical halves hinged like a clamshell with two long handles; a spring cradle with spurs; toothed jaws crack the shell in two; on opening, contents fall out and the cradle springs throw off the shell. Parts include spurs, jaws, latches and springs.

### US 546,255 — Automatic egg-boiling apparatus (Sheet 1, Fig. I)
- "the same quantity of water may discharge in two, three, four, and six minutes" — description, operation (multiple nozzles)
- "so long as this remains the case the egg-basket will remain lowered" — description, operation
- "the bucket with its contents becomes heavier than the egg-basket and its contents" — description, operation
- "the operator always knows to a certainty that the valve is correctly set into either position" — description, multiple-valve section (stop studs)
- Facts: egg basket in boiling water hangs on a chain over a pulley/wheel with an empty bucket on the other end; water drips from an overhead tank into the bucket through a chosen nozzle; when the bucket outweighs the basket it drops and lifts the eggs out; the mechanism "at the same time shuts off the discharge of water" (description, opening) / "the flow of water through the cock 31 is cut off" (description).

### US 1,513,685 — Combined alarm clock and article heater (Sheet 1, Figs. 1–2; sheet printed sideways, rotated 90° for display)
- "A further object is that of providing a clock having means adapted to give an alarm at a set hour," — description, objects
- "the lamp is illuminated, and the preparation of the coffee is started and continues" — description, operation summary
- "When the cup 65 is about full of coffee," — description, operation (continues: the weight of the cup and coffee overbalances weight 13 and lever 15 is rocked)
- "the coffee percolator ceases operation, the light of the lamp goes out, the alarm is sounded a second time" — description, operation summary
- Facts: Fig. 1 front view — clock at the bottom, percolator above, lamp dome on top; coffee goes into cup 65 on tray 11; tray on a lever with a counterweight; full cup cuts the power.

### US 3,670,642 — Combination toasting and buttering apparatus (Sheet 1, Fig. 1)
- "where it may be baked onto the heating elements" — description, prior art (earlier toasters applied shortening above the heating compartment; excess dripped down)
- "so the toast slice 25 is buttered and sliced as it falls downwardly through the lower compartment 15." — description
- "the apparatus then cycled a couple of times to spray warm soapy water through the nozzle 63 to clean it." — description
- Facts: bread enters at the top and toasts on a retractable fork; a timer withdraws the fork, the toast falls into the lower compartment, a motor-driven pump/piston sprays melted shortening (butter) and a chain-driven blade slices it in half.

### US 5,481,963 — Automated pancake maker (Sheets 1, 4, 5, 6; Figs. 1, 4, 5, 6)
- "automatically cooking, flipping, monogramming and discharging a plurality of pancakes" — description, background (field)
- "In many restaurants where the cooking grille surface is zealously guarded, pancakes are not served." — description, prior art
- "to dispense the batter out the exit like toothpaste" — description, brief description of the invention
- "total entrapment of half-cooked pancakes 37 during all flipping and transfer exercises" — description, advantages
- "the apparatus can be made to be coin operated" — description, closing
- Facts: three heated dishes; pancakes need higher grill temperatures than other grill foods (prior art: "Pancakes require much higher temperatures than do other grille cooked foodstuffs"); batter pumped through flexible rubber tubes; second heated plate pivots over, helped by a falling deadweight, locks on, both rotate to flip; plate tilts and pancakes slide into a tray (Fig. 6). Drawings show the pancakes lettered "Good Mornin" (Figs. 1, 5, 6).

### US 1,324,659 — Fruit-eating shield (drawing sheet, Figs. 1–2)
- "the manipulation of a spoon therein causes the juice to be ejected and sprayed to great disadvantage" — description, background
- "so as to form a pocket under which a spoon is manipulated" — description (hood 7)
- "the eatable or pulp portion of the fruit is visible by reason of the transparency of the shield" — description, operation
- Facts: transparent glass disk on a cut half grapefruit; flanges grip the fruit; hood 7 forms a spoon pocket; finger ring 6 lets one hand grasp fruit and shield ("a hand finger is inserted therethrough in grasping the fruit with the same hand").

### US 1,661,036 — Grapefruit shield (drawing sheet, Fig. 1)
- "for preventing the spattering of the juice when eating the fruit" — description
- "thrown away after a single use" — description, closing
- "may be used to bear advertising matter" — description, closing
- Facts: tall hood of flexible (waxed) paper anchored by tines in the rind ("adapted for anchorage in the skin or rind of the fruit", claim 1). Issued Feb. 28, 1928 — nine years after US 1,324,659 (1919).

### US 4,986,433 — Crispy cereal serving piece and method (Sheet 1, Figs. 1 and 3)
- "Soggy cereal is so undesirable that many people will not eat it" — description, background
- "simply discard the soggy cereal and its milk, which can be wasteful and costly" — description, background
- "the cereal eventually becomes soggy anyway." — description, background (treated cereals that claim to stay crisp only retard absorption)
- "The slope of the chute then causes the measured portion of cereal to slide under the influence of gravity" — description
- "the weighted base 13 helps to counteract the forces generated by the chute" — description (base "preferably hollow and filled with a weighted material such as, for example, sand")
- "no matter how leisurely a breakfast he enjoys" — description
- Facts: lower bowl of milk, raised upper bowl of dry cereal, sloped chute between; a portion "quickly consumable in two or three bites" is pushed down with a spoon; repeat.

### US 4,092,093 — Apparatus for deforming boiled egg (Sheet 1 Fig. 1; Sheet 2 Figs. 7 and 10)
- "changing a whole boiled egg into an aesthetic cubic shape" — description, objects
- "troublesome and time-consuming to change the original natural shape of the boiled egg into a desired shape by cutting it with a knife" — description, prior art (full sentence starts "It is, however, …")
- "is required to be equal to a cubic root of the volume of an egg." — description (side of the square chamber)
- Fact: "According to the experiments conducted by the inventor, there were obtained a very good result … when, especially, said length of each one side was 3.9 cm" — description
- "the boiled egg 20 is progressively pressed by the pressing surface 13 of the pressing member 3" — description
- "the yolk 21 itself will be deformed substantially into a cubic shape as shown in FIG. 7" — description
- "it is desirable to cool the egg-containing apparatus in water or in a refrigerator" — description
- "Whereupon, the deformed boiled egg will easily come out from the main body 2." — description
- "a triangular, a square, a rectangular, a pentagonal, a hexagonal shape, or a round shape" — description
- "desired indented letters or symbols onto the surface of this boiled egg" — description, objects
- Facts: peeled egg put in hot or at least warm; main body "may be made with a transparent plastic material" (so the egg may be viewed externally); threaded cap presses a piston; bottom plate with an indented pattern (Figs. 8–10; Fig. 10 = finished cube with a pattern on top).
