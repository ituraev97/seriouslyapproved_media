My invention relates to toys, and the object
thereof is to provide a toy drum-beater, con-
sisting of a figure, preferably that of a mon-
key, with internal mechanism so constructed
that by operating the same in the manner de-
scribed the figure may be made to beat the
drum or other device, the mechanism being

soarranged that at each movement or opera-

tion thereof the regular time or marching

-beat is produced, which may, however, be va-

ried as desired. This object I accomplish by

‘means of the construction described in the

following specification, of which the accom-
panying drawings form a part, in which—

5, also composed of sheet metal and of the
