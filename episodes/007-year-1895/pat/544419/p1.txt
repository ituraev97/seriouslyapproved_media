Our invention relates toan improvement in
electric-wire-containing bands for hats and.
caps, the object being to produce a simple,
convenient, and effective removable band
adapted to have an incandescent light at-

tached to it as well as body-wires for supply- .

ing a current thereto.

With these ends in view our invention con-
sists in an electric-wire-containing hat or cap
band provided with terminals for the remov-
able attachment of an incandescent electric
lamp and of body-wires for supplying an elec-
tric current thereto.

Our invention further consists in certain
details of construction and combinations of
parts, as will be hereinafter described, and
pointed out in the claims.
