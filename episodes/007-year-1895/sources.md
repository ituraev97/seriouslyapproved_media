## Quotes and facts per patent
### US 531,878 — Cane-umbrella (p1–p2)
- "The object of this invention is to simplify the construction and operation of cane umbrellas" — description, opening
- "the other end of the cane can remain permanently closed" — description ("does not require any temporary closing device such as a removable plug, or screw cap or the like")
- Knob 14 "forms its top when the cane is used for walking and its bottom when the umbrella is open" — description (spoken paraphrase-quote)
- Facts: closed umbrella inserted into the hollow cane until rim 13 of plug 12 strikes the edge of the cane; catch 11 holds it; to open: release catch, withdraw, reverse, plug F enters the case, push stick → opens. Figs: 1 open, 2 closed (in cane).

### US 533,149 — Fan attachment for swings (p1)
- "a simple and cheap attachment adapted to fan the occupant of a swing during the oscillation of the swing" — description, objects
- "the fan 8 will swing in the direction contrary to that the occupant of the swing moves, and, as is obvious, will fan him" — description
- "the occupant of the swing would derive no benefit from air thrown off from the fan in other than a downward direction" — description
- "a needless amount of motion on the part of the fan makes a swing needlessly hard to operate" — description
- "whether it be an ordinary seat, a cradle, a hammock, or so on" — description ("I herein use swing in the broad sense of swinging support, …")
- Facts: Fig. 1 portable support (four standards), Fig. 2 stationary support "suspended by the ropes 11, 11 from trees or the roofs of porches"; fan 8 on fan-handles 7, 7 hinged to the frame; driver 3 moves contrary to the swing.

### US 535,674 — Attachment for bicycles (p1–p2)
- "enables the bicycle to be run with great ease, speed, and safety, even by an inexperienced rider, upon the rails of an ordinary railway track" — description, object (on screen: "upon the rails of an ordinary railway track")
- "a device of great simplicity and cheapness, which may be applied to any safety bicycle" — description, object
- "may be folded into a very small compass" — description ("which when separated from a bicycle may be folded into a very small compass")
- "enables the machine to be practically self-steering" — description
- "all the rider need do is to simply use his strength to propel the machine, and the machine will follow the rails like an ordinary car" — description
- Facts: small third wheel 14 runs on the opposite rail; concaved rim 14a "to enable it to hug the rail"; three braces 17, 18, 19 to the steering fork and front/rear axles; each brace in two parts joined by pipe coupling 24, held by set screws 25 (longitudinal adjustment); Fig. 1 shows the bicycle on rails.

### US 543,874 — Velocipede hobby-horse (p1–p2)
- "My invention relates to a hobby-horse velocipede" — description
- "when the hobby-horse is rocked forward the sprocket-wheel 13 is rotated, and also when the horse is rocked rearward said wheel is rotated" — description
- "the forward portion of the hobby-horse may be moved up and down" — description (steering, Fig. 5 dotted lines)
- Facts: semicircular tooth-racks 16 and 17 rigidly secured to the hobby-horse; cog-wheels 14 with ratchet 15 (one turns the spindle only one way, the other "in a reverse manner"); sprocket 13 → sprocket 20 on rear axle 2, wheels 3 fixed to the axle; front steering wheel 22; rod 29 passes through the horse with handle 30 on top.

### US 544,419 — Electric-wire-containing hat or cap band (p1–p2)
- Fig. 1: "a perspective view of a policeman's hat provided with a band constructed in accordance with our invention" (Google text: "policemans", OCR artifact; spoken only, not shown as a quote card)
- "adapted to have an incandescent light attached to it" — description, object
- "Under this construction the lamp may be attached to and removed from the band with perfect convenience." — description
- "composed of enameled leather, or otherwise constructed, so as to be waterproof" — description
- "lead down from the hat or cap over the body to some source of electrical energy" — description (body-wires E, E')
- "a dynamo carried upon a bicycle, or what not" — description (scan: "or what not"; Google text capitalises "What" — OCR artifact) ("whether that be a battery carried in the pocket, or …")
- "The hat or cap will otherwise be worn just the same as any ordinary hat or cap" — description
- Facts: forward member A with tab A' and yielding contact-buttons a, a' for lamp D in reflector D' (Fig. 7); rear tab B' with buttons b, b' for the body-wires; band adjustable "to fit any particular hat or cap".

### US 546,021 — Toy (drum-beater) (p1–p2)
- Assigned "by direct and mesne assignments, to the Waterbury Button Company, of Waterbury, Connecticut" — heading of the text page (not shown)
- "a toy drum-beater, consisting of a figure, preferably that of a monkey" — description
- "each drum-stick striking the drum at each movement of the bar 12" and "the regular time or marching-beat of the drum" — description
- "being slid back and forth slowly or quickly, as desired, a moderate movement being preferable" — description, operation
- "which will readily attract the attention and which is comparatively inexpensive and not easily injured" — description, end
- Facts: body of thin metal, coat/skirt 5, handle 6 with drum 7 on its curved end, rack-bar 12 sliding inside, pinion 11, crank levers 14 move the arms 17, drum-sticks 20.

### US 545,958 — Toy (climbing spider) (p1–p2)
- "taking up the string as a spider does his web" — description ("thus causing the insect to apparently climb by taking up the string as a spider does his web")
- "the legs of the insect in the climbing vibrating in a rapid and natural manner" — description
- "so that the spider will seem to be trying to catch it" — description (imitation fly 15)
- "imparts, probably, a more life-like appearance to the movements of the legs" — description, Fig. 4 ("but it is more complex and expensive than that shown in the principal views")
- Facts: spring-barrel 2 with coil spring 3 "similar to the spring of a watch"; crank-pin 9 vibrates legs 12; the patent calls the spider "the insect" throughout; Fig. 4: two slotted plates 11*, three legs on each.

### US 546,105 — Marine velocipede (p1–p2)
- "two cigar-shaped cylinders or floats arranged parallel to each other" — description ("A A are two cigar-shaped cylinders or floats arranged parallel to each other")
- "may be fixed an umbrella, flag, or other convenient device, at pleasure" — description (staff H)
- "there are no obstructions to become clogged with vegetable or other floating matter" — description
- Facts: body B supported "at the rear by beams C and at the front by the spring C'"; wheel-house D for the propelling-wheel at the rear; steering-post with handles G, T-head, cables K to crosses J and two rudders M.

### US 546,104 — Canopy for bicycles (p1–p2)
- "for protecting the rider from the rays of the sun and to shelter him from rain" — description, object
- "may be arranged at an angle, to operate as a sail for accelerating the speed of the bicycle to assist the rider" — description, object
- "it offers no obstruction in riding against the wind" — description, end
- "may be arranged to assist the rider when the bicycle is traveling in the direction of the wind" — description, end
- "In applying the canopy to a lady's bicycle the clamps are attached to the steering-bar." — description
- Facts: "compactly folded when not in use"; stem 14 clamped to the frame; Fig. 7 modification: horizontal bars 27 and vertical bars 28 on the stem.
