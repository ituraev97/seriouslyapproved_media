[57] ABSTRACT

A fork with timer comprising a fork having a head,.a
plurality of spaced tines extended from the head, and a
handle extended from the head remote from the tines;
timer circuitry connected to the handle of the fork and
adapted for providing a cue after an elapsed period of
time for indicating to user that another bite of food
using the fork may be taken; a replaceable power source
connected to the fork and coupled to the timer circuitry
with the power source adapted for energizing the timer
circuitry; and a switch connected to the fork and cou-
pled between the power source and the timer circuitry
with the switch having one orientation for energizing
the timer circuitry and another orientation for de-ener-
gizing the timer circuitry.
