[57] ABSTRACT

A new toilet tank assembly aquarium for housing aquatic
creatures. The assembly includes a toilet bowl assembly
with a toilet tank assembly coupled to the toilet bowl
assembly. The toilet tank assembly has a top, a front, a back,
and a pair of sides. The front, back and sides of the toilet
tank assembly define an interior space with the top of the
toilet tank assembly having an opening into the interior
space of the toilet tank assembly. A toilet water reservoir is
located in the interior space. A lid substantially covers the
opening of the top of the toilet tank assembly. The front of
the toilet tank assembly is generally transparent.
