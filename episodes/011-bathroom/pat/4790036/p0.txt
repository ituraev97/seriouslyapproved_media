[57] ABSTRACT

A toilet seat (27) is provided with a slide housing (26)
comprising nozzles for spraying and/or applying water
and/or disinfecting liquid. It comprises at least one
wiper for drying the surface of the seat (27). The slide
housing (26) is placed over a segment of the seat (27),
which then, while being sprayed by the nozzles, is
driven in the circumferential direction by at least one
electric motor. The entire operation is started up manu-
ally by actuating the lever (2) of the flushing water
cistern (1), and subsequently is controlled by the control
electronics (27) until the cleaning of the seat (27) has
been completed. The energy is obtained by a turbine
(11), which is mechanically connected toa low-voltage
direct-current generator (13), and is driven by the water
filling the flushing water cistern (11). The actual flush-
ing water, therefore, flows at the normal kinetic energy
into the toilet; the hydraulic resistance of the turbine
(11) is not switched on while the flushing water flows
into the toilet bowl. The device is extremely reliable,
does not carry any dangerous electric voltages, and in
addition contains safety measures which prevent possi-
ble injuries, in particular of children. It is suitable for
use with new installations, as well as for adaptation to
already existing toilets.
