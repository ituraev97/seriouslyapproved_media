My invention relates to bath tubs.

The object of my invention is to provide
a rocking bath tub having means to prevent
the splashing of water during the rocking
action.

A further cbject of the invention is to
provide a device of this kind having a
curved bottom and having suitable rockers
secured thereto.

The preferred embodiment of my inven-
tion is hereinafter described and illustrated
in the accompanying drawings.

In these drawings:—Figure 1 is a side
view in elevation. Fig. 2 is an inverted plan
view, and Fig. 8 is a detail perspective
view of a portion of one of the rocker mem-
bers.

rounded bottom 2. The bottom 2 at 3 is
