[57] ABSTRACT

A table specialized for toilet use has a spindle with
exceptional access supporting a roll of toilet paper, a
tabletop supported by legs at a height and an angle
optionally usable by one seated in the ordinary way on
a flush toilet seat. An edge retains objects such as read-
ing material on the inclined table top, and a platform
with spaced slats supports reading material and the like
between floor and toilet paper roll. The roll retracts
easily when pushed, so that any selected side of the
table can be stored pushed against a wall.
