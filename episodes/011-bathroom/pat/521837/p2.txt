It will be seen in Fig. 2 that the sides of
the tub are eurved inward toward their top,
and for this reason the standards extend to
the top of the tub. It is necessary in this
structure that the said standards have their
upper ends —d’— on both sides formed in
separate parts fromthe body thereof. Other-
wise it would be impracticable to introduce
the body B of the outer casing into the stand-
ards to be fixed therein, as shown, because
the swell of the body is greater than the dis-
tance between the tops of the standards in
their normal position. But by making the

frame in sections on the line of the swell of
the body we are enabled to introduce the
body into the standards, and after this is
done to introduce the ends —d’— thereof.
