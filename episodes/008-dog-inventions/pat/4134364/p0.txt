[57] ‘ABSTRACT

A dog tether for a wheeled vehicle such as a bicycle
comprising a stand-off bar which has a clamp at its inner
end for attachment to the bicycle frame, and a resilient
mounting for the clamp yieldable to take up any sudden
jerk by the dog. The outer end of the bar has an adjust-
able chain or wiffle-tree attachment for respectively
tethering one or more dogs.
