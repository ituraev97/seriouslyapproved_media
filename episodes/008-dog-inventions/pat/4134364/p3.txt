DOG EXERCISING DEVICE.,
DISCUSSION OF THE INVENTION

This invention is directed toa dog tethering structure 5

which positions the dog alongside the bicycle in a posi-
tio. where the animal is undef control of the vehicle
and prevents the dog from running into the vehicle or
running off sidewise, endangering the rider.

The invention comprehends providing a novel at-
tachment which is easy to apply'‘and remove and which
has an adjustable tether for different size animals.

The invention contemplates in addition to a resilient
mounting for the stand-off bar, a releasable securing
latch which yieldably resists normal tugging by the dog
and which releases if the dog should suddenly lurch
with great force such as might endanger the bicycle
rider.
