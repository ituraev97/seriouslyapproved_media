FAA STATEMENT · NOV 14, 2026
“The wingtip of a Copa Airlines Boeing 737 struck the nose of a UPS Boeing 767 while the airplanes were taxiing at Miami International Airport.
Air traffic controllers had told the UPS crew to give way to the Copa flight.”
FAA: around 9 p.m. local time · Airport: about 9:15 p.m.
NTSB · AS QUOTED BY GLOBAL NEWS, NOV 15, 2026
UPS 358 was taxiing to the runway and was instructed to give way to the Copa 737 (HP-1826CMP), which was taxiing to the gate.
“UPS358 did not stop, and the left winglet of CMP427 impacted the nose of UPS358.”
Substantial damage to the UPS airplane · Copa damage assessment not yet completed
