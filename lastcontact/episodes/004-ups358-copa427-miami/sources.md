# LC004 — UPS 358 (B767-300F N336UP) / Copa 427 (B737-800 HP-1826CMP) taxiway collision, MIA, Nov 14 2026 — sources
- FAA statement, Nov 14 2026 (accident/incident statements page): Copa 737 wingtip struck nose of UPS 767 while taxiing; ATC had told UPS crew to give way to the Copa flight; Copa taxiing to gate, UPS taxiing for departure to Bogota, Colombia; ~9 p.m. local; FAA investigating. https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/statements/accident_incidents
- NTSB statement (Nov 15) as quoted by Global News: UPS358 N336UP taxiing to runway, instructed to give way to B737 HP-1826CMP taxiing to gate; "UPS358 did not stop, and the left winglet of CMP427 impacted the nose of UPS358"; substantial damage to UPS airplane; Copa damage assessment not yet completed; prelim within 30 days. https://globalnews.ca/news/12152916/2-injured-copa-airlines-passenger-jet-ups-cargo-plane-collide-miami-airport/
- AP via PBS NewsHour (NTSB "substantial damage", prelim within 30 days; Copa "minor"; UPS: "We cannot speculate on the details", NTSB "is responsible for investigating incidents like this"). https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/2-planes-collide-on-the-ground-in-miami-only-minor-injuries-reported
