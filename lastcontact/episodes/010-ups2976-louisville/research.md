# Research notes — episode 010

Why this case: highest current demand on the topic (YouTube search, US/EN: top UPS 2976 videos at 1.54M, 0.90M, 0.44M and 0.19M views, including the NTSB hearing coverage) and a rich official record: NTSB preliminary report, Jan 14 2026 update, May 19-20 2026 investigative hearing docket, NTSB animation. Investigation is still open, so the episode is built as "what the NTSB has found so far" and never states a cause.

Key facts used (all from the NTSB record): takeoff roll 17R, liftoff 17:13:05 EST at ~174 kt; engine 1 data unreliable ~4 s after liftoff; fire indication ~6 s; max radio altitude 29 ft at +16 s; increasing left roll at +20 s; end of data at +25 s; fractured aft mount lugs and spherical bearing race; fatigue and overstress areas on the race; Boeing service letter MD-11-SL-54-104 (May 2008, rev. 2011); seven operator reports of a cracked bearing before the accident (Boeing list, exhibit 7F, as of April 2026); N259UP ~92,992 h / 21,043 cycles; UPS grounded its MD-11s three days after the crash; UPS inspections found three MD-11s with a migrated bearing race (exhibit 7K); hearing May 19-20 2026 with two panels; Chairwoman: hope for answers no later than May 2027.

Not used: cockpit voice recording content (chosen), crew/victim names.
