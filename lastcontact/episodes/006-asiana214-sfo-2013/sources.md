# LC006 — Asiana Airlines flight 214, B777-200ER HL7742, SFO RWY 28L, 2013-07-06 — sources
Primary: NTSB Aircraft Accident Report NTSB/AAR-14/01 (adopted June 24, 2014), https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/AAR1401.pdf (text extracted with pdftotext; line refs in fact-check).
- Executive summary / 1.1: date, time ~1128 PDT, 291 pax + 12 FA + 4 flight crew = 307; visual approach to 28L; vectors; 180 kt to 5 nm instruction at 1123:17 (~14.1 nm); LOC capture 1122:52 (15.4 nm, ~5,300 ft, ~210 kt); 1124:53 "this seems a little high", "do you mean it's too high?", "I will descend more", V/S −1,500 at 1125:04; 5 nm at 1125:55, ~400 ft above 3° path, 174 kt; flaps 30 called 1126:12 at 174 kt (> 170 kt limit); FLCH SPD 1126:25.7, A/P off 1126:28.8, thrust levers idle / A/T HOLD 1126:33; 1,000 ft RA 1126:54.9, 2.1 nm, 151 kt, 1,500 fpm, PAPI WWWW, 243 ft above 3° path; Table 1 (all last-1,000-ft events, RA and airspeeds); 500 ft RA 1127:14.8 137 kt, 1,200 fpm vs ~700 needed, N1 ~24%; lowest airspeed ~103 kt at 1127:46.9; go-around call 1127:47.8; impact 1127:50 (Table 1: 1127:50.3), N1 ~92%, ~106 kt; tail separated at aft pressure bulkhead; spun ~330°; rest ~2,400 ft from seawall, stop ~1128:06; footnote 25 engine spool-up 6–7 s, 8 s spec; footnote 17 PAPI colours; footnote 23 not a check flight.
- 1.2 Table 2: 3 fatal, 49 serious, 138 minor, 117 none.
- 1.5: PF age 45, 9,684 h, 33 h 777; PM 12,307 h, 3,208 h 777, first flight as IP; observer = relief FO.
- 1.7: METAR 1056/1156 wind 210/6–7, 10 mi, few clouds 1,600 ft.
- 1.10: RSA project, 28L threshold displaced 300 ft, 600-ft RSA seawall→threshold, glideslope out of service (NOTAM June 1, 2013), PAPI flight-checked July 2, 2013 at 2.98°.
- 1.12: MLG and aft fuselage struck seawall.
- Exec summary / 1.15: slide/rafts inflated in cabin (two FAs injured, temporarily trapped); six ejected incl. 4 FAs (restrained, aft galley destroyed) and 2 of 3 fatally injured passengers (no seatbelts; would likely have survived); fire started in separated right engine; 98% self-evacuated; firefighters extricated five passengers; 99% survived.
- 2.1: weather suitable for visual approach; ATC speed restriction per FAA procedures; no preimpact structural/engine/system failures, no AFCS problems. 2.2.2: PF likely selected FLCH SPD. 2.5.1: first visual approach without ILS glideslope in the 777 outside the simulator. Fatigue analysis: 8 time zones, normal sleep time, likely fatigued. Finding 8: insufficient monitoring of airspeed likely from expectancy, workload, fatigue, automation reliance. Finding 10: PF inaccurate understanding of AFDS/A/T interaction. ~97% of unstable approaches continued (industry statistics).
- 3.2 probable cause + 5 contributing factors (quoted verbatim).
- 4: recommendations A-14-37 … A-14-63 (27).
